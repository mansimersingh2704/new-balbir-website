About Us

Decoration crafted with care, right here in Lucknow

NEW BALBIR LIGHT HOUSE has been turning ordinary spaces into memorable celebration venues across Lucknow. From intimate family functions to grand wedding festivities, we handle every detail of the decor — lighting, tenting, floral work and stage design — with a premium, personal touch.

Based in Alambagh, our experienced team works closely with every family to design themes that reflect their taste and budget, arriving early and setting up on time so you can simply enjoy the celebration.
